Vegetable Fried Rice

Cooking Time: 20 minutes

Warning: Be careful while handling hot oil.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 carrot, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup chopped beans
  • 1/2 cup green peas
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Add chopped carrots, beans and green peas.
  3. Cook the vegetables until slightly tender.
  4. Add cooked rice, salt and pepper.
  5. Mix everything well and cook for 2–3 minutes.
  6. Serve hot.
Nutrition Facts

Calories: 250 kcal

Protein: 6 g

Carbohydrates: 40 g

Fat: 8 g
