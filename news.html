Scientists Discover a New Planet

Scientists have discovered a new planet called Gliese 12 b, located about 40 light-years from Earth. It is similar in size to Earth and Venus and takes only 12.8 days to orbit its star. Scientists are studying this planet to understand whether it can retain an atmosphere and potentially support habitable conditions.
